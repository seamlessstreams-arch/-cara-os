# Writing to the child — before/after examples

## Example 1

**Before:**

we spoke about your review meeting and what you want to say in it. you did really good at putting your views across

**After:**

Suggested child-conscious wording for practitioner review (write this with, and for, the child):

This is what happened, in plain words: we spoke about your review meeting and what you want to say in it. you did really good at putting your views across
What adults did to help: [describe what staff did, calmly and kindly].
What happens next: [describe the next step you agreed].
Still to add for the child: What did the child say or show? Include their voice — or record carefully how they communicated without words.; Include human detail — how was the day for the child, not only what was done?; What did staff do to help? Record the adult response, not only the child's behaviour..
